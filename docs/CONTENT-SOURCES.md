Research date: 22–23 October 2026 (Asia/Kuala_Lumpur).

## Redirects from the current website

`src/lib/redirects.mjs` maps 37 existing isp.edu.my paths (About Us, Learning, Admissions, School Life, Community) to their new equivalents with permanent redirects. Unmapped URLs show the 404 page with search and popular links rather than redirecting everyone to the home page. Review the map with the digital owner and add any other indexed URLs before switching the domain.
